## The Main Rule

**Your brand leads. Arc is the infrastructure.**

Arc can be used to explain what your product is built on, what network you support, or where your integration is available.

It should NOT become your product name, app identity, logo system, or implied endorsement.

---

## Approved Language

Use Arc in these descriptive contexts only:

- "Our app is built on Arc"
- "Available on Arc"
- "Our app supports Arc"
- "We are live on Arc"

---

## Language to Avoid

Never use:

- "Arc by [Your Company]"
- "The Arc App"
- "Arc Payments"
- Any phrasing that makes Arc sound like your product, company, or commercial endorsement

If the relationship needs context, say what the relationship actually is.
Do not make the Arc name or logo carry more meaning than the approved relationship supports.

---

## Logo Usage Rules

- Use the Arc logo ONLY if you are actively building on Arc OR have a signed partnership agreement with Circle
- Use the latest logo files from the Circle Brand Kit
- Do NOT modify the mark, recolor it, distort it, recreate it, place text or graphics over it, or make it more prominent than your own branding
- The logo should help explain the infrastructure relationship — it should NOT compete with your product identity

---

## Name Usage Rules

- Use Arc in descriptive contexts: documentation, partner pages, launch materials, and UI copy where you need to show that your product is built on or supports Arc
- Do NOT incorporate Arc into your company name, product name, app icon, or brand system
- When needed, use "Arc Network" on first mention and "Arc" after that

---

## Approvals and Questions

- Circle may review, request changes to, or revoke use of Arc Brand Assets at any time
- For partner launches, product pages, co-marketing assets, paid media, or anything that could imply endorsement or a relationship beyond what has been approved, check the guidelines first
- Official guidelines: https://www.arc.io/brand-guidelines-and-partner-toolkit

## ARCTIS-Specific Application

### ARCTIS is the product. Arc is the infrastructure.

| Context | Correct | Incorrect |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Product identity | ARCTIS | Arc / ARC |
| Network reference | "Built on Arc" | "Arc-powered ARCTIS" |
| Currency | "Arc Native USDC" | "Arc USDC by ARCTIS" |
| Network name | "Arc Testnet" | "ARCTIS Arc" |
| Explorer | "ArcScan" (proper noun, acceptable) | — |

### What is ALWAYS acceptable in ARCTIS UI
- "Arc Testnet" — factual network name
- "Arc Native USDC" — factual token description
- "Built on Arc" — approved infrastructure language
- "ArcScan" — proper noun for the explorer
- Chain ID 5042002 — technical fact

### What requires review before use
- Arc logo in marketing materials
- Any claim of official Arc/Circle partnership or endorsement
- Co-branded assets

### What is NEVER acceptable
- Presenting ARCTIS as an Arc product
- Using Arc as part of the ARCTIS brand identity
- Making Arc logo more prominent than the ARCTIS identity
- "Arc by ARCTIS" or similar constructions
